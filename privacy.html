Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026 · northpointysa.com and the North Point YSA Ward text list

Who we are

The North Point YSA Ward is a young single adult congregation of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in the Roswell Georgia Stake, meeting in the Atlanta area. This website (northpointysa.com) and our text list are run by the ward's local volunteer leaders to help members take roll, get announcements, and reach ward leadership. They are not operated by the Church's central offices; official Church privacy information is at churchofjesuschrist.org/legal/privacy.

What we collect

  • Check-ins. When you check in for a class on the roll page, we record your name (from the ward membership list), the class, and the date. Visitors who type a name are recorded the same way.
  • Text list. When you join the ward text list, we collect your mobile number and the name you give us, plus the date you joined and your opt-in choice.
  • Notes to leadership. If you use the housing, jobs, or "meet with the Bishop" forms, we keep what you send — typically your name, a phone number or email, and your message.
  • Membership records. Ward leaders see names, contact details, callings and move-in dates from the Church's membership records so the roll works and new members get welcomed. That information comes from Church records you have already provided to the Church, not from this site.
  • The game. If you save a score in "The keys", we store the name you type, your score and the date. Choose any name you like.
  • Technical basics. Like any website, our hosting provider logs standard request information (such as IP address and browser type). We do not run advertising trackers or sell analytics.

How we use it

  • To take roll and report class attendance in the Church's membership system, as the ward is asked to do.
  • To send ward announcements, reminders and event information by text message and email.
  • To help ward leaders extend callings, welcome new members and follow up with people who have asked to meet with the Bishop or asked for help.
  • To keep the leaders' pages secure and the site working.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Text messages

By joining the North Point YSA Ward text list you agree to receive recurring informational text messages (ward announcements, activity reminders, Sunday information, and occasional messages from ward leaders) at the number you provided. Messages may be sent using an autodialer. Message frequency varies, up to 20 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at the address below. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of attending the ward or of any Church membership or service. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except to the service provider that delivers the messages on our behalf.

Who we share it with

Only ward leaders who need it (the bishopric, clerks, and organization leaders) can see the leaders' pages, behind a passphrase. Beyond that, your information is handled by the services we use to run the site:

  • SimpleTexting delivers our text messages and stores the text-list numbers.
  • Supabase hosts the site's database (check-ins, notes, settings, game scores).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website itself.
  • Google (Sheets, Forms and Gmail) holds leaders' working notes and sends email from ward leaders.
  • The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints membership system (Leader and Clerk Resources), where attendance is recorded.

These providers process the information only to provide their service to us. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Attendance stays in the Church's records under its own policies. Text-list numbers stay on the list until you reply STOP or ask to be removed. Notes to leadership are kept while leaders are working on them and cleared when handled. Game scores stay on the board until removed. You can ask us to delete anything we hold about you on this site at any time.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Leaders' pages require a passphrase, and the database only answers the specific requests the site needs. No system is perfect, but we keep the amount of information we hold small and limit who can see it.

Children

The North Point YSA Ward is for adults ages 18 to 35. This site and text list are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to leave the text list.
  • Ask any member of the bishopric, or write to us below, to see, correct or delete what we hold about you on this site.
  • Questions about Church membership records themselves go through the ward clerk.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update it here with a new effective date.
