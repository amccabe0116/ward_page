About this site

northpointysa.com is run by the volunteer leaders of the North Point YSA Ward (Roswell Georgia Stake) of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, for the members and friends of the ward. It lets you check in for class, read announcements, join the ward text list, and send a note to ward leadership. It is a local ward tool, not an official website of the Church. By using the site or the text list you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy.

Text message program

Program name: North Point YSA Ward text list.

What you'll get: recurring informational messages from ward leaders — weekly announcements, activity and Sunday reminders, and occasional messages from the bishopric or organization leaders. This is not a marketing program.

How you join: by texting the ward number, by filling out our sign-up form, or by asking a ward leader to add you. Joining means you consent to receive these messages at the number you give us.

Frequency: varies, up to 20 messages per month. Messages may be sent using an autodialer.

Cost: message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. We do not charge for the list.

To stop: reply STOP to any message and you will be removed. You may get one final message confirming you've been unsubscribed.

Help: reply HELP, email npysawardclerk@gmail.com, or talk to any member of the bishopric.

Consent is not a condition of attending the ward or of any Church membership or service. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Messages are sent through SimpleTexting on our behalf.

Using the roll

The roll page is for recording your own attendance. Please check in only yourself (or a visitor you have brought, when they ask you to). Leaders may correct attendance records and report them in the Church's membership system.

Notes to leadership

Messages you send through the housing, jobs, or "meet with the Bishop" forms go to ward leaders. Please keep them honest and respectful, and don't include anyone else's private information without their permission. Leaders will use them only to help you.

"The keys" game and the high score board

The game is for fun. Scores you save appear on a board that anyone visiting the page can see, under the name you type. Use a name you're comfortable showing; ward leaders may remove any entry.

Acceptable use

Don't try to access the leaders' pages without authorization, tamper with the roll, submit false or abusive content, or use the site in a way that interferes with others' use of it.
