What the tags mean
Warning
Attending a YSA ward is optional — we're telling them that if they don't start showing up, their records go back to their home ward. Send the warning message on their slide texts/emails them and records the date.
Magnet
Their records came in without a new-member meeting, so they're being sent back to their previous ward. Send the magnet message does the same for this case.
Move Aged out
From a note in parentheses after the name on the sheet, e.g. "Shane Woods (aged out)". Other Notes itself is just text — nothing is read out of it.
Nothing yet Proposed Texted Answered / Declined Accepted / Sustained
Where the calling stands, from the Proposed / Who texts / Texted / Answer / Sustained columns.
new not on sheet
new = moved in within the last 30 days (LCR's move-in date; it drops off by itself). not on sheet = on LCR's report but with no row on the sheet yet — Add to sheet on their slide (or Add all on this filter) makes the row, pre-filled from LCR and their move-in form.
Has a calling?
On the sheet and still on the ward roll, but no longer on LCR's Members without Callings report — so a calling was most likely recorded in LCR. Check their Callings/Classes tab in LCR; once it's done, Remove on their slide takes them off the sheet.
Not in LCR
On the sheet but not on the ward roll at all — their records moved out (or were never here). Remove deletes the row.
Ready to move out
The warning / magnet message went out long enough ago (21 days for Warning, 7 for Magnet — change it under Settings) that it's time to move their records. The date comes from the "Flag sent" column, which is filled in automatically when you send from here or can be typed in.
✎ pending
Edited here on the site; it goes into the Google Sheet the next time the sync runs.
Removed Left off the sheet
Remove on a slide takes someone off this list: their row is deleted from the Google Sheet and they stay hidden until LCR stops listing them (records moved, or a calling recorded). Leave off the sheet does the same for someone who has no row on purpose (with a note saying why) — they stop counting as “not on sheet” and Add all skips them. Both are under the Removed / left off filter, where Back on the list undoes it.

How the LCR sync works

Attendance taken on this site is pushed into LCR's Class and Quorum Attendance report by a script that runs inside your logged-in LCR tab (it clicks the same buttons you would). The roster is refreshed from that page at the same time.

  1. Open Class and Quorum Attendance in LCR and sign in.
  2. Run scripts/lcr-sync.js in that tab (Claude does this for you on Sunday nights, or on request: “sync attendance to LCR”).
  3. Rows below turn synced once LCR has them.

Only weeks LCR still allows editing can be pushed (LCR opens each week through the coming Sunday).

Check-in windows

Warning / Magnet messages

What Send the warning / magnet message on a person's slide sends. {first} = first name, {name} = full name. Please read these through as a bishopric before the first send.

Text list sync (new member form → SimpleTexting)

Whoever ticks agree on the new member form’s “Automated Messages” question (and gives a phone number) is added to the SimpleTexting list, with the form date kept on their contact as the consent record. It runs after each sheet sync (every 6 hours), never re-adds anyone who replied STOP, and never removes anyone.
