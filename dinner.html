Dinner with the missionaries

Sign up, get paired with someone, take the missionaries out to eat.

Here’s how it works: sign up below, and we’ll pair you with someone in the ward — a random match, close to your age. The two of you pick a night, and take the missionaries out for a meal. Good food, a new friend, and the missionaries fed. Everybody wins.

What gets shared. Your match gets your first name and phone number so you can plan the night; the missionaries get your names. Nothing else goes anywhere.
